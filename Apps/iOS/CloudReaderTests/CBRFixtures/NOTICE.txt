rar4-absolute-directory.rar is derived from the Unrar.swift 0.5.4
multibyte.v4.rar fixture above. Its zero-length directory header was changed to
the absolute path /evil/ and its RAR4 header checksum was recalculated.

rar4-solid-skip-unrelated.rar is derived from the CC0 RAR4 image fixture above.
The first member was renamed from testfile.jpg to testfile.txt, the following
PNG member's solid flag was set, and both RAR4 header checksums were recalculated.
This lets the test verify that skipping a non-page member leaves solid extraction
state usable for the next image.
