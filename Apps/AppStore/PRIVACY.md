# CloudReader Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 22, 2026**

CloudReader is an offline-first book library. It does not require a CloudReader account, and the developer does not operate a server that stores your library or AI conversations.

## Information stored on your device

CloudReader stores the books you import, library details, reading progress, favorites, reader settings, and any AI conversations you choose to save. Provider connection settings and credentials are stored on your device; credentials are kept in the operating system’s secure credential store. These items are not sent to the CloudReader developer.

## Optional sync to storage you choose

If you configure sync, CloudReader transfers your selected library data to the storage destinations you choose. This can include book files, titles and other library details, reading progress, favorites, deletion records, and saved AI conversations. Supported destinations vary by platform and include services such as Google Drive, Dropbox, pCloud, WebDAV, SFTP, iCloud Drive, and user-selected file locations.

CloudReader does not operate these storage services. Copies stored there are governed by the provider’s terms and privacy policy. Removing a book in CloudReader removes its local copy and synchronizes a deletion record to connected devices, but does not currently erase the corresponding book file from remote storage. Delete that file directly through the provider if you want it removed there. Providers may retain backups or version history under their own policies.

## Optional AI features

Apple’s Foundation Model runs on device when supported by the device and available in the user’s system settings. If you configure a remote AI API, CloudReader sends your submitted question to the endpoint you configure. Book text is included only if you choose to attach a passage before sending. Requests go directly from your device to that endpoint; the developer does not receive them. The provider’s terms and retention practices apply to remote requests.

CloudReader saves AI conversations to your local library after you ask a question. If sync is enabled, those conversations and any included book context are synchronized to your selected storage destinations.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

CloudReader does not include advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking, or a CloudReader-operated account or library service.

## Your choices and deletion

You can use the local library without configuring sync or remote AI. You can remove books and saved conversations from CloudReader. Data already sent to a storage or AI provider is subject to that provider’s controls and retention policy; manage copies in the provider’s service as needed.
